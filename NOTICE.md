# Code, data, and attribution

We release our study-specific code and documentation under the MIT license. That license does not replace the terms or attribution requirements of the precipitation products, HURDAT2 tracks, watershed boundaries, state boundaries, or software dependencies used by the workflow.

We have not bundled the full precipitation archives or proprietary basemap imagery. Data access instructions and the identity of the products we used are provided in `docs/DATA_ACCESS.md`. Small derived tables, selected boundary files, and figure-ready arrays are identified in their accompanying provenance records. Their source attribution remains applicable.

We cite the TC/ETC and EC method papers in `docs/METHODS.md`. We preserve provenance for the study scripts in the repository inventory. Standard libraries and installed dependencies retain their own licenses; they are not redistributed as our code.

We credit Miguel Diaz as the repository author in `CITATION.cff`. The public repository URL and any archived software DOI will be added when available. The citation is not a claim that the manuscript has been published.
