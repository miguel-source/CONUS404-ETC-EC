Figure S4. We evaluated transformation sensitivity by comparing estimated latent-signal R² from raw precipitation with estimates from log1p-transformed precipitation for CONUS404 and CONUS404BA in core and expanded windows. We used identical cells satisfying full admissibility in both triplets under both transformations; no bootstrap screening was applied. Each point is a grid cell and diamonds mark watershed-area-weighted means. The dashed line marks equal numerical R². The transformations define different statistical representations of precipitation and associated latent signals, so departures from the diagonal show sensitivity rather than an accuracy improvement. We compare dimensionless R² values, not raw error SD in mm day⁻¹ against transformed-unit error SD.
